    Frequently Asked Questions

    How much does a mezzanine floor cost in Melbourne?

    Cost depends on the floor area, load rating, column spacing, decking, stairs, gates and handrails. We prepare a detailed quote once we understand your space, starting from photos or sketches and confirmed at a free site consultation.

    Do I need a site visit to get a quote?

    Not initially. We can prepare a preliminary proposal and quote from drawings, photos or rough sketches of your space. We recommend a free site visit to make sure the design suits your workflow, and a final site inspection is mandatory before fabrication.

    How long does mezzanine installation take?

    Our crews typically install 50 to 100 square metres per day, so most mezzanine floors are installed within a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on size and complexity.

    Can I expand or relocate my mezzanine in the future?

    Yes. Our modular mezzanine floors are designed for flexibility, so they can be extended or relocated as your business changes.

    Which areas of Melbourne do you service?

    We design and install mezzanine floors across metropolitan Melbourne and regional Victoria. That includes the western industrial suburbs of Derrimut, Truganina, Laverton North, Altona North, Sunshine West, Ravenhall and Brooklyn; Tullamarine, Campbellfield, Thomastown, Epping, Somerton and Preston in the north; Port Melbourne and Richmond closer to the city; and Dandenong South, Keysborough, Braeside, Moorabbin, Clayton, Mulgrave, Bayswater, Hallam and Carrum Downs in the south-east.
